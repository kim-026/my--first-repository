How to Play 75-Ball Bingo

1. The Numbers & Columns:
75 balls are distributed across 5 letter columns:
• B: 1 – 15
• I: 16 – 30
• N: 31 – 45 (Center space is FREE!)
• G: 46 – 60
• O: 61 – 75

2. Daubing:
Click or tap matching numbers when they are called. Enable Auto-Daub if you'd like the system to daub automatically for you.

3. Winning:
Once you complete the target pattern (e.g., any row, column, diagonal, or 4 corners), the CALL BINGO! button will flash. Click it to claim your victory and trigger the celebration fanfare!

4. Party Host Mode:
Switch to the Party Caller & Board tab to display the full 75-number master board on a TV or projector for family and friends party game nights.
